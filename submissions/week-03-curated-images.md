# Week 3: Curated Images for Portfolio

## Image Requirements Map
Based on the identity kit and portfolio structure, here are the images needed:
1. **Hero Texture / Background:** Subtle, dark, technical backdrop to add depth without distracting.
2. **Profile Photo:** A professional headshot or candid for the "About" section.
3. **Project Case Studies:** Visual proof of work for the portfolio grid and individual case study pages.
4. **UI Icons / Connective Tissue:** Small consistent icons for navigation and skills.

## The Keepers (Final Image Set)

### 1. Connective Tissue (AI Generated)
For the hero background, I generated options keeping the aesthetic "highly technical and calm" with near black and subtle zinc/calm blue accents. 

**Selection:** The subtle geometric grid texture.
*Why:* It provides a deep, immersive background that reduces eye strain (`#0A0A0A` theme) and matches the precise typography focus without competing for attention.

### 2. Work & Identity (Real Captures)
* **Profile Photo:** **REAL PHOTO.** I chose a real, high-quality headshot of myself. AI generation here would feel inauthentic and break the trust a portfolio needs to build.
* **Project Screenshots (Case Studies):** **REAL CAPTURES.** I used clean, cropped screenshots of the actual Next.js application and React components. AI stand-ins for technical work defeat the purpose of "proof of work." The assignment is to show what I built, not what an AI thinks an app looks like.

## The Rejection Note (Discernment)

**Rejected Image:** A generated glowing blue cyberpunk circuit board texture.
*Why I rejected it:* While it fits the "dark mode" theme, it is way too loud and complex. The portfolio's style note explicitly calls for the interface to act as a "quiet frame" that lets the engineering work shine. This image would overwhelm the content and make the site feel like a gaming blog rather than a professional developer portfolio. I chose the minimal geometric grid instead because it serves the content rather than competing with it.
